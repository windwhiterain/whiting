# 筇竹 — Qiongzhuea tumidinoda

*Measured 2026-10-18. One question: what does this bamboo look like, and how does it grow?*

> **Taxonomic warning for the modeller.** FRPS and the Chinese literature accept **Qiongzhuea** as a genus separate from *Chimonobambusa*, and describe *Q. tumidinoda* as 筇竹. The GBIF backbone instead lists this name as a **SYNONYM placed in *Chimonobambusa*** (key 4145698, canonical name *Qiongzhuea tumidinoda*, genus *Chimonobambusa*). Also rejected as names: *Chimonobambusa tumidinoda* (comb. invalid) and *C. tumidissinoda* (nom. invalid). **Photographs and literature are split between the two generic placements**, so a search by either name returns partly different image sets. This note uses the FRPS treatment throughout.

## Site

- **Altitude:** the species has no standalone range in FRPS, but its vertical belt is given explicitly: **1560–2100 m**, reaching **2200 m** at most. Below 1400 m the same slopes carry *Phyllostachys*, and 1400–1560 m carries *Chimonobambusa* — so 筇竹 is a **narrow, clearly bounded altitudinal band**, not a generalist.
- **Provinces:** 四川宜宾地区 (Yibin, Sichuan) and 云南昭通地区 (Zhaotong, Yunnan) — the **northeast rim of the Yunnan–Guizhou Plateau where it drops toward the Sichuan Basin**, a subalpine transition zone (亚高山地带).
- **Vegetation belt:** subalpine evergreen broadleaf forest. FRPS: 大面积集中成片生长于山区上部到山脊的常绿阔叶林 — in large, dense, contiguous stands from the upper slopes to the ridges, forming the **main understorey tree** (主要下木) in sites at Suijiang, Weixin, Yiliang (Yunnan) and Junlian (Sichuan).
- **Stand density:** **8 000–17 000 culms per 亩** (1 亩 = 666.7 m²), i.e. roughly **12–26 culms per square metre**. This is a dense, closed thicket, not a scattering of open clumps.

## Habit

- **Culm height: 2.5–6 m.** Culm diameter **1–3 cm.**
- **Silhouette, one sentence:** a dense, tall, narrow, dark-green thicket of near-vertical slender culms whose swollen, clasped-disc nodes give every culm a visibly knotted, bead-like rhythm — the "knotty-jointed bamboo".
- **Growth form:** large contiguous stands (see Site). FRPS describes the underground part as **5 internodes below the soil surface, each bearing a ring of 12 adventitious roots** (基部通常有5节位于地表以下，各具环列之根12条) — a rhizome with a ring of roots at every buried node.
- **Culm lean:** FRPS does not state a lean. *Estimate:* culms are held **erect or leaning only slightly outward with age**; both node photographs kept below show near-vertical culms with no arching.

## Branching

- **Branches per node: usually 3** (竿每节通常具3枝), with **1–4 secondary branches in addition to the main branch** sometimes present.
- **Which nodes branch:** **every node along the branched part of the culm** — this is not a bamboo that branches only at the top. The lower, non-branching internodes are a distinct zone near the base.
- **Branching plane — the modeller's key fact:** the internodes **below** the branching zone are cylindrical but carry **one extremely narrow groove, and the groove is on the same side of every internode** (各节间的沟槽均位于竿之同一侧面). Internodes **at** branching nodes **flatten on the side the branches emerge from** (在有分枝一侧变扁平). So the culm has a consistent flat face that alternates with branch positions along its length.
- **Buds:** 3 main buds that, after the sheath drops, **do not lie flat against the culm but spread obliquely** (并不贴竿而是斜展的主芽3), each with 1–2 secondary buds beneath.
- **Twig leaves:** **2–4 leaves per twig** (小枝具2-4叶).

## Culm

- **Diameter: 1–3 cm.**
- **Internode length: 15–25 cm**; the lowest few internodes are shorter, **10–15 cm**.
- **Wall: very thick (竿壁甚厚).** The **lowest internodes are almost solid (几为实心)** and hollow only gradually upward. A modeller should model a nearly solid, very thick-walled base that becomes a thin-walled tube by mid-culm.
- **Surface: green, smooth, glabrous, with no wax bloom** (绿色，光滑无毛，无蜡粉).
- **Node shape — the diagnostic, and the whole reason for the name *tumidinoda*:** the node ring is **extremely raised, forming a prominent rounded ridge shaped like two discs clasped one above the other, with a shallow ring-shaped seam-like groove between them** (状如二圆盘上下相扣合，中有环形缝线似的浅沟). The culm **breaks brittlely at these rings under lateral force, node by node, with an extremely flat fracture face**.
- **Sheath ring (箨环):** slightly woody from the remains of the sheath base, covered in brown bristles when young and glabrous later.
- **Culm sheath (箨):** purplish-red or purple with green (紫红色或紫色带绿), **early-deciduous** — it drops soon after the shoot extends, which is why photographs show clean, smooth, green culms.
- **Sheath proper (箨鞘):** yellow-green, thick papery, **about half the internode length**, long-elliptic, slightly narrowed at the base and drawn out into ear-like side projections; back densely and prominently longitudinally veined; **brown wart-based bristles between the veins, sparse at the sheath base and denser toward the top**, the upper margin fringed with pale brown long cilia; **no auricle**; sheath mouth with 2–3 mm brown setae.
- **Sheath blade (箨片):** small, **5–17 mm**, deciduous, awl-shaped to narrowly lanceolate, erect and tough, visibly veined.
- **Ligule (箨舌):** 1–1.3 mm high, arched, fringed with grey-white cilia.

## Foliage

- **Leaf type:** simple, **narrowly lanceolate (狭披针形)**.
- **Size: 5–14 cm long × 6–12 mm wide.**
- **Margin:** **rough, with small oblique-pointing teeth** (两侧边缘因具斜止之小锯齿而粗糙) — not entire.
- **Colour:** **green above, grey-green beneath** (上表面绿色，下表面灰绿色); both surfaces glabrous.
- **Venation:** 2–4 pairs of secondary veins, cross-veins clear.
- **Petiole:** **1–2 mm**, smooth and glabrous.
- **Leaf sheath:** cylindrical, **2–2.5 cm**, with one longitudinal ridge on the back; **no leaf auricle**; sheath mouth with several erect, rough, grey-white, easily detached setae. Leaf ligule very low, truncate or arched, entire.
- **Evergreen:** yes — the stands are described as evergreen broadleaf-forest understorey and are photographed fully in leaf.
- **Distribution along a shoot:** leaves cluster at the twig tips in twos to fours; the twigs themselves arise three at a node from the culm.

## Reproductive

- **Flowering:** April (April–May shoots; flowers April, fruit May).
- **Pseudospikelets:** green or dark green, **purple when dry**, **3–4.5 cm long × 2.5–4 mm thick**, 4–5 bracts, each spikelet with **3–8 florets**; flowering branches repeatedly branched, partly leafless, and often mixed with the pseudospikelets on the same node.
- **Anthers:** **purple**, 4–8 mm long.
- **Fruit:** a thick-coriaceous nut, obovate-oblong or broadly elliptic, **ink-green and smooth when fresh, ca. 10–12 mm long × 6 mm across**, with the persistent style as a beak at the tip.
- **Note for the model:** bamboos flower rarely and unpredictably, and *Qiongzhuea* is a wild, still-forest bamboo — flowering is not a reliable seasonal feature of its appearance. Do not model flowers or fruit into the routine silhouette.

## The five facts that let you recognise this bamboo from 200 m

1. **The bead-like nodes.** Every culm is ringed at close intervals by a **strongly raised collar like two clasped discs, with a seam groove** — no other bamboo in that forest reads this way. This is the species' name in a single feature.
2. **Slender, near-vertical, green culms 2.5–6 m tall and only 1–3 cm thick.** The ratio of height to diameter is extreme — a thicket of these looks like a stand of canes, not canes.
3. **An unbroken line of nodes with no taper to the eye** — internodes hold 15–25 cm for most of the culm length, so the vertical rhythm is regular, like a bamboo pipe rather than a tapering stem.
4. **Dense, contiguous thickets, not scattered clumps.** 12–26 culms per square metre in large stands from upper slope to ridge; from a distance the mass has a solid, blocky, dark-green texture.
5. **A soft, drooping, grey-green leaf mass** — long narrow leaves (to 14 cm) hanging in loose sprays and arching outward from the culms, greyer beneath. The upper canopy of a 筇竹 stand looks feathery and slightly untidy, not spiky or rigid like the conifers it grows under.
