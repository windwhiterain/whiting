# 滇润楠 — Machilus yunnanensis

*Measured 2026-10-18. One question: what does this tree look like, and how does it grow?*

## Site

- **Altitude: 1500–2000 m** for var. *yunnanensis*.
- **Habitat:** **montane evergreen broadleaf forest** (山地常绿阔叶林). Prefers **moist, fertile mountain slopes** (喜湿润和土壤肥沃的山坡).
- **Provinces:** central, western and northwestern Yunnan; western Sichuan.
- **Rooting:** **deep-rooted** (为深根性树种), and grows well.
- **Wood:** an excellent building and furniture timber.

## Habit

- **Mature height: to 30 m. DBH to 80 cm.** Sub-canopy to emergent tree of the montane evergreen forest.
- **Silhouette, one sentence:** a tall, straight-trunked evergreen with a broad, rather open, dark crown of well-spaced glabrous leaves that catches light as a mottled glossy canopy.
- **Branches cylindrical, with longitudinal striations, green when young and brown when old, glabrous.**
- **Crown change with age:** *Estimate:* conical when young, broadening with age; the widely spaced leaves (疏离) keep the crown open throughout.

## Branching

- **Leaves alternate and **widely spaced (互生，疏离)** — the shoot looks airy, with clear gaps between successive leaves rather than a crowded spiral. This is the branching-and-foliage character to reproduce.
- **Phyllotaxy:** alternate. **First-order branch angle:** *not stated*; *estimate:* 45–75°, spreading.
- **Inflorescence position:** the panicles are borne **mostly on the lower part of the short shoots** (生于短枝下部) — a real placement fact, useful for where to hang flowers.
- **Lower branches:** *Estimate:* shed from the base with age, leaving a clean bole.

## Trunk

- **Form:** straight, cylindrical.
- **Bark:** *not described in this entry* — FRPS gives the branch surface as longitudinally striated, green young / brown old. *Estimate:* grey to grey-brown, smooth on young stems, slightly fissured on old trunks.
- **Not spiny.**

## Foliage

- **Leaf type:** simple, alternate, **coriaceous (革质)**.
- **Leaf size: (5)7–9 (12) cm long × (2)3.5–4 (5) cm wide.**
- **Shape: obovate or obovate-elliptic, occasionally elliptic** (倒卵形或倒卵状椭圆形，间或椭圆形) — widest above the middle, tapering to the base.
- **Apex: shortly acuminate with a blunt tip** (短渐尖，尖头钝). **Base: cuneate, sometimes asymmetric on the two sides.**
- **Margin: cartilaginous and rolled under (软骨质而背卷)** — a thick, recurved edge, a strong identification character.
- **Colour:** **upper surface green or yellow-green and glossy; lower surface pale green or glaucous-green** (淡绿色或粉绿色). Both surfaces glabrous.
- **Venation: 7–9 lateral veins per side**, sometimes forked, arched, and **raised on both surfaces**; the transverse and fine veins are reticulate and **form conspicuous honeycomb-shaped pits (蜂巢状窝穴) on both faces**. That honeycomb texture is a usable surface detail on the leaf.
- **Midrib:** slightly sunken in its lower part on the upper surface, nearly flat above, **distinctly raised beneath**.
- **Petiole: 1–1.75 cm**, **grooved on the ventral side and rounded on the back**, glabrous.
- **Evergreen:** yes — it is a defining member of the montane evergreen broadleaf forest.

## Reproductive

- **Inflorescence:** composed of **1–3-flowered cymes (1-3花的聚伞花序)**; sometimes the upper or all cymes of the panicle bear a single flower, giving a **false raceme**. Panicle **2–9 cm long (usually 3.5–7)**, mostly on the lower part of short shoots; peduncle 1–3.5 cm; all axes and pedicels glabrous.
- **Bracts:** broadly ovate to nearly orbicular, **5–8 mm**, the outer bracts smaller, **densely rust-coloured hairy outside**, nearly glabrous inside; **bracteoles linear, to 4 mm × only 0.3 mm**, rust-hairy outside. Bracts and bracteoles are **early deciduous**.
- **Flowers: pale green, yellow-green or jade-white, 4–5 mm long**; pedicel 4–10 mm. Perianth tube obconical, about 1 mm; **perianth lobes oblong with acute tips, outer whorl slightly shorter (3.5–4 mm), inner whorl 4–4.5 mm and under 2 mm wide.**
- **Stamens:** filaments hairy at the base; the third whorl slightly longer, with **a pair of stalked round-reniform glands at the filament base, the stalk half the filament length**.
- **Gynoecium:** ovary ovoid, glabrous, 1.5 mm; style filiform, about as long as the ovary; small capitate stigma.
- **Fruit: ellipsoid, about 1.4 cm long, with a small point at the apex, black-blue and glaucous (with a white bloom) when ripe, glabrous.** The **persistent perianth lobes do not enlarge and are reflexed**; the pedicel does not thicken, about 1.2 mm at the tip.
- **Season:** flowers **April–May**; fruit **June–October**.

## The five facts that let you recognise this tree from 200 m

1. **A tall, straight, clean bole lifting a broad, open crown.** The outline is a slim column widening suddenly — the montane evergreen forest's characteristic profile.
2. **An airy, mottled canopy, not a solid one.** The leaves are **widely spaced on the shoots** and the blades are glossy above and pale glaucous beneath, so the crown shimmers with light and dark patches and you can see sky through it.
3. **A crown of large, blunt-ended leaves.** The individual blades are big (to 12 cm), obovate, and stop in a soft blunt point rather than a drip tip — so the foliage texture is coarse and chunky rather than fine.
4. **Pale undersides flashing.** The glaucous-grey-green lower surface turns the whole canopy silver-grey when wind lifts it, which is the most conspicuous moving feature of the tree.
5. **Dark blue-black berries in loose clusters.** Ellipsoid fruits, 1.4 cm, ripening black-blue with a white bloom on 1–3-flowered cymes low on the short shoots — small dark beads among the pale leaf undersides from June to October.
